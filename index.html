About Me

I’m the Head of Research at Stripes, building the diligence and investment research team to enable us to make investments in incredible software and consumer companies.

I was previously a Consultant at the New York office of Bain & Company working within the Private Equity Group (PEG) to perform commercial due diligence on SaaS and fintech assets for VC, Growth Equity, and Crossover clients.

Prior to that, I served as the Head of Growth at Simon Data, an enterprise customer data platform, from Seed to Series C. Additionally, I’ve served as an independent investor, consultant and/or advisor to several early-stage SaaS companies in NYC working with companies like Cedar Health, Klara, Maestro, Eigen, Ethena, and others.

I am a graduate of Columbia Business School and was a Second Year Fellow and the recipient of the Glenn Hubbard Scholarship. During my time at Columbia, I was a VC Fellow with General Catalyst, an MBA Associate at Primary Venture Partners, a Student Fellow at .406 Ventures, a Venture Partner at Contrary Capital, and an InSITE Fellow.
