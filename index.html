About

I’m the Principal and Head of Research & Growth Strategy at Stripes, building the diligence and investment research team to enable investments in incredible software and consumer companies.

Previously, I was a Consultant at the New York office of Bain & Company within the Private Equity Group (PEG), performing commercial due diligence on SaaS and fintech assets for VC, Growth Equity, and Crossover clients.

Prior to that, I served as the Head of Growth at Simon Data, an enterprise customer data platform, from Seed to Series C. Additionally, I’ve been an independent investor, consultant, and advisor to several early-stage SaaS companies in NYC including Cedar Health, Klara, Maestro, Eigen, Ethena, and others.

I am a graduate of Columbia Business School and was a Second Year Fellow and the recipient of the Glenn Hubbard Scholarship. During my time at Columbia, I was a VC Fellow with General Catalyst, an MBA Associate at Primary Venture Partners, a Student Fellow at .406 Ventures, a Venture Partner at Contrary Capital, and an InSITE Fellow.
